# Grand Theft Azeroth

**Turn WoW: Forever into a crime-city comedy.** Download: [CurseForge](https://www.curseforge.com/wow/addons/grand-theft-azeroth). Wanted stars, a rolling cash counter, mission banners, stolen rides, a fake phone, a radio with made-up stations, hit contracts, rampages and a pile of random nonsense. Everything reacts to things you already do in game.

It's a parody and purely cosmetic: it never plays the game for you, never posts to public chat, and every gag can be switched off.

## Open the phone

Nobody remembers slash commands, so everything sits in the **Murloc Mobile** phone menu:

- Left-click the **minimap button** (right-click for random chaos)
- Or click the cash and stars display
- Or bind a key in *Options > Keybindings > AddOns*

The phone has apps for the radio, chaos, contracts, cheat codes, your criminal record, achievements, missed messages, a demo and settings.

## What happens

- **Death screen**: big slanted FLATTENED / DELETED / YEETED titles, or NICKED if the cops were after you, plus a hospital bill.
- **Missions**: MISSION PASSED on quest turn-in with respect and confetti, MISSION FAILED when you abandon one, HEIST COMPLETE on boss kills.
- **Wanted stars**: attack guards, critters or players, trespass on enemy turf, get slapped by an emote. Stars fade when you lie low and clear at an inn or when you hearth.
- **Cash counter**: your gold as a rolling dollar total with a cha-ching.
- **Stolen rides**: a vehicle card when you mount, a speedometer in murlocs per hour, a taxi meter on flight paths.
- **Radio**: three made-up music stations and a talk station, auto-playing while mounted.
- **Turf**: zone and subzone cards with taglines, HEIST BRIEFING in dungeons, GANG WAR in battlegrounds, a night shift.
- **Contracts and rampages**: shady contacts text you hit contracts based on what you've been killing; kill fast for a RAMPAGE.
- **Career**: earn respect, climb from Street Rat to Legend of the Streets, unlock 20 career achievements.
- **Random chaos**: breaking news tickers, police patrols, tax audits, speed cameras, birds, insults from passers-by. Choose how often with the chaos level (0 to 3).
- **Cheat codes**: purely visual, all on buttons in the phone.

Text pop-ups never interrupt a fight: they wait until combat ends, and the Messages app keeps the last ones.

## Commands

`/gta` lists everything. Useful ones: `/gta phone`, `/gta demo`, `/gta chaos 0-3`, `/gta toggle <gag>`, `/gta errors`.
