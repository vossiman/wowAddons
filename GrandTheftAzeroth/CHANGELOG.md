# Changelog

## 0.7.0-beta

Collections and discovery: finding something new is now a moment.

- **TURF DISCOVERED** when you uncover a new area, with a running count and respect
- **EXPANDING OPERATIONS** on new flight paths, counting your safehouse network
- **NEW PRODUCT LINE** for recipes (a trainer haul shows as one message)
- **NEW ASSOCIATE RECRUITED** for companion pets, **NEW WHIP IN THE GARAGE** for mounts (cutscene and confetti), **CONTRABAND ACQUIRED** for toys
- **NEW FIT** sticker for transmog appearances (at most one a minute, plus wardrobe milestones at 10, 25, 50 and every 100 per day)
- **STREET NAME EARNED** for titles, **NEW SKILL SET** for newly learned skills
- New **Collection** app in the phone: garage value, associates, contraband, wardrobe value, turf, safehouses, product lines and street names
- 5 new career achievements, and a "Collections & discovery" switch in settings
- Combat stickers for hits on your target only show when it's your fight; if the target is fighting your group, crits show as a small CREW CRIT at the screen edge; other people's fights are ignored
- The Messages app now also lists stickers and banners, with the code line that caused each one

## 0.6.0-beta

Everything now reacts to what you actually do. Only the breaking-news ticker still runs on a timer.

- **Contracts are your real quests**: kill and loot objectives from your quest log show in the tracker as hit contracts and pickup jobs, with +1 stickers, TARGET ELIMINATED / PACKAGE SECURED, and a cutscene when the job is done. Click the tracker (or the Switch job app) to change quest
- **Wanted level from your kill rate**: 3 / 5 / 8 / 12 / 16 kills per minute give 1 to 5 stars; the rate shows next to the stars. No more random "crime" stars
- **Subzones do things**: a subzone card, plus place-based happenings (inn hides you, banks get cased, farmers yell, confession booths clear your heat, muggers, vendors, graffiti)
- **Capitals**: DOWNTOWN in your own capital (heat fades faster), ENEMY DOWNTOWN in theirs (+2 stars, heat fades slower)
- **Combat stickers**: CRIT!, OOF, DENIED, NOPE, NOT TODAY, DODGED, PARRIED, BLOCKED, WHIFF
- **Skill-ups** show as a sticker with the skill and rank, with a banner every 25 points
- **Loot**: first green and blue of the day, JACKPOT on epics, SAFE CRACKED on chests and lockboxes, PRODUCT ACQUIRED when gathering, MANUFACTURED when crafting, FENCED when vendoring
- **Movement**: falls (SPLAT), driving into lakes, the Coast Guard after a long swim, joyride bonus, speed cameras when riding flat out, loitering downtown, birds when you stand around outside
- **Fights**: WALKED IT OFF at low health, UPSET OF THE YEAR on higher-level kills, long fights cool your heat, group wipes make the news, your pet getting whacked
- **Social**: trades, inspects, guild and group invites, whispers on a burner phone, dance-offs and radio dedications
- **Character**: lunch breaks, DUI and public intoxication, fully rested, new talent points and spells, fresh threads, repairs, hearthstone ready again, and a "Previously on Grand Theft Azeroth" recap after a break
- **Phone texts** now arrive after deaths, missions, level-ups, rampages, cities, taxis, banks and wipes instead of on a timer
- The old random events (patrols, audits, insults, celebrities, wrong-person arrests, tips, fake achievements) now each have a real trigger
- Breaking news: 88 satirical headlines (crime, city hall and war, markets, society, health, weather and traffic, the media, real estate, sports, horoscopes, ads, live reports), many using your name, class, zone, recent kills and stats. No repeats until all have run
- Combat stickers sit in a ring around your character; WHIFF means you missed, TOO SLOW means they missed you

## 0.5.5-beta

- Much less noise: by default only deaths, missions, level-ups, rank-ups, heists, rampages and gang wars play a sound. No more pings and blips on every pop-up, star or coin
- "All sounds" app in the phone (and a settings checkbox) brings every gag's sound back
- Cheat codes and the demo still play their sounds, since you asked for them
- Skill-up pop-up now names the skill and rank (it used to just say "Skill increased" for any skill)

## 0.5.3-beta (first public release)

- Phone menu ("Murloc Mobile") with a minimap button, key binding and addon-list entry
- Death screen, mission banners, wanted stars, cash counter, stolen rides, speedometer, taxi meter
- Radio with three music stations and a talk station
- Zone and subzone cards, heist briefings, gang wars, night shift, hearthstone rides
- Hit contracts, rampages, respect ranks and 20 career achievements
- Random chaos events with a chaos level from 0 to 3
- Visual-only cheat codes
- Text pop-ups wait until combat ends; recent ones are kept in the Messages app
- Volume setting (off, low, med, high) in the phone; sounds follow the game's Sound Effects volume, the radio follows the Music volume
- Every frame can be shift-dragged and remembers its spot; a click closes pop-ups like the news ticker; Layout and Reset layout apps in the phone
